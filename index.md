# BalloonX Support

**How to play:** Tap balloons to pop them before they float off the top of the screen. Each regular balloon that escapes costs a life; you start with 5.

| Balloon | Effect |
|---|---|
| ⭐ Gold | +5 points |
| ❤️ Heart | +1 life (max 5) |
| ❄️ Ice | Freezes all balloons for 3 seconds |
| 💣 Bomb | Don't pop it — costs a life (letting it float away is safe) |

**Pause:** Tap the ⏸ button at the top. The game also pauses automatically if you leave the app.

**Sound:** Use the speaker button on the start screen, or Sound in the pause menu. Sound also follows your iPhone's Silent switch.

**Reset your best score:** Delete and reinstall the app.
