# BalloonX Privacy Policy

_Effective date: November 24, 2026_

BalloonX ("the app") is a single-player game. We respect your privacy.

## Information we collect
**None.** BalloonX does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information or usage data. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking, and it does not connect to the internet.

## Information stored on your device
The app saves two settings **only on your device**: your best score and whether sound is on or off. This information never leaves your device and is deleted when you delete the app.

## Children's privacy
Because BalloonX collects no information from anyone, it collects no information from children under 13.

## Changes to this policy
If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
